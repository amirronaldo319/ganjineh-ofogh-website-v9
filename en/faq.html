Questions and answers

What people ask.

Short answers, taken only from the information supplied for the project. What is not yet confirmed is said so.

The project

Where is the project?

Tehran, Ozgol, Golshan Sharghi Street, at the Parvin intersection, the start of Parvin Street, No. 6. This is the supplied address; the Location page has a schematic map and the main routes.

Project location

How large is the site, and how much of it is green space?

The site is 2,064 m², with more than 1,400 m² of landscaping and 700 m² of shared spaces. These figures are as supplied and will be checked against the approved project records.

About the project

Who is building it?

The project is by Ofogh Group. A selection of the group's building record, from 1350 to 1404 SH, is on the Ofogh Group page.

About Ofogh Group

Are the images real?

No. The images are architectural visualisations and the 3D models are concept reconstructions. Finishes, layouts and areas are indicative and subject to approved project records.

The residences

How many apartments are on a floor, and how large are they?

Each typical floor has four three-bedroom apartments of 143.25, 150, 166 and 162 m² (Apartments 1 to 4). The areas are as supplied.

Compare two apartments

Can I see inside an apartment before it is built?

You can walk through furnished 3D models of all four apartments and explore the interactive tower model. They are concept reconstructions; furniture and room divisions are illustrative.

View the residences

How many floors does the tower have?

The number of floors and the heights are not yet confirmed by approved drawings. The 3D tower model is built on assumptions that are listed in its Sources and assumptions panel.

The 3D tower model

Does the roof have a helipad?

The architectural images show a helipad on the roof. The image is not an approval of a permit or of use; use of the roof is subject to approved project records.

Architecture

Shared spaces

What shared spaces does it have?

A celebration hall, a cinema, a fitness space, a café and library, and a social lounge. Their final positions follow the approved plan.
