Design approach

Light. Calm. Connection.

Natural light, a clear arrival and a connection to planting shape the residential concept. Soft textures and restrained colours bring continuity to the interior.

A home is not only a place to live; it is part of the quality of every day.

Ganjineh Ofogh was conceived beyond the making of a residential complex: a place where contemporary architecture, the calm of nature and the quality of everyday life sit side by side.

From the way you arrive to the outlook of each residence, from shared spaces to interior details, every part is designed for a coherent, calm and lasting experience.

Architecture

Three principles, one architectural expression.

A balance between proportion, light and a continuous connection with nature.

01

Proportion

The rhythm of the façade and the proportion of openings balance architectural presence with human scale.

02

Light

Spaces are arranged around the entry of natural light and a better interior environment.

03

Connection with nature

Terraces, outlooks and planting strengthen the link between interior and surroundings.
