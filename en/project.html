Vision

A home beyond an address.

Ganjineh Ofogh was conceived beyond the making of a residential complex: a place where contemporary architecture, the calm of nature and the quality of everyday life sit side by side.

From the way you arrive to the outlook of each residence, from shared spaces to interior details, every part is designed for a coherent, calm and lasting experience.
