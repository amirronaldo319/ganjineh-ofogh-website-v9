# Captions for the social cards

Cards are in `launch/social/fa/` and `launch/social/en/`, each in three sizes: `square` (1080×1080, Instagram feed), `story` (1080×1920, stories and reels covers), `wide` (1280×720, Telegram and link previews). Regenerate with `node tools/build_social.mjs`.

Every caption below uses only what the website already says. Add the real phone number, handle or price yourself once they are approved: none is included here.
