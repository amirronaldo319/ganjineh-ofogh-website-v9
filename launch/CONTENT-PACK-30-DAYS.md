# 30 days of content (Instagram · Telegram · Eitaa), Persian + English

Built only from what the website already says. **No price, delivery date, floor count, orientation or availability appears,
because none has been supplied.** Areas are "as supplied" (143.25 / 150 / 166 / 162 m²; see B1 in `CONFIRM-IN-5-MINUTES.md`).
Persian lines need a native read before posting. Assets are files already in the repo (`assets/img/…`, `launch/social/…`).

## Rules for every post
1. Concept renders carry the words «تصویرسازی مفهومی» / "concept render" on the image or in the first caption line.
2. The helipad is only ever shown as a concept («مفهومی؛ تأیید مجوز نیست»).
3. Areas: «بر پایه‌ی اطلاعات ارائه‌شده» / "as supplied".
4. One call to action per post, and only to something that works: the website, the 3D model, or (once live) the enquiry form.
5. Reply to comments with facts from the site; if unknown, say "the team will confirm", never guess.

## Rhythm
Mon plan · Tue 3D model · Wed lifestyle · Thu location/architecture · Fri apartment spotlight · Sat story (day→dusk) · Sun quiet (re-share).
Formats: **R** reel 15–25 s · **C** carousel · **S** story set · **P** single image.
